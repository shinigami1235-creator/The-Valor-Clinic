Our Services

Metabolic Care

Prevention is better than cure. Early detection before symptoms appear helps to prevent complications and improve the chances of a successful outcome.

A metabolic care clinic is a medical facility that specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of metabolic disorders involving problems with the way the body produces and uses energy, including cardiovascular diseases, endocrine disorders, fatty liver and obesity. Patients at the metabolic care clinic will receive a range of services, including medical evaluations, nutrition counseling and medication management. The goal of treatment at the metabolic care clinic is to help patients better manage their conditions and improve their overall health and well-being.

Heart

Cardio-Monitoring Services

At Valor Clinic, we believe that digital health technology in combination with mankind’s expertise will revolutionize the way of clinical practice and allow for more accurate and comprehensive monitoring of health. We have a board-certified cardiologist who has been trained as an interventional cardiologist from an international country.

Wearable devices

“S-Patch” is a non-invasive and portable device designed to track heart rate, electrocardiogram, breathing range and other metrics in real-time.

Convenience

An S-patch can be worn on the chest wherever you go during whatever activities you do.

Continuous monitoring

The heart rate and electrocardiogram can be tracked continuously. The more accurate and comprehensive data collection we have, the better chance of early diagnosis is.

Early warning

An S-patch can alert you to any potential problems with your heart rate or other vital signs, which can allow you to take action to address the issue before it becomes more serious.

Remote monitoring

S-patch can transmit the data to healthcare provider in real-time. By using AI to analyze initially, cardiologists can identify patterns and trends that may indicate potential problems with your heart health. Without the need for in-person visits, this service can be especially helpful for anyone who live in remote areas or who have difficulty traveling.

Liver

Liver Care Services

A liver care program aims to assess and manage liver health in individuals. At Valor Clinic, we use FibroScan™, a non-invasive FDA-approval medical device that uses ultrasound technology to measure the stiffness or elasticity of the liver, which can provide information about the health and structure of the liver.

With FibroScan™, we can assess the severity of liver damage or disease in individuals, such as liver fibrosis (scarring) or cirrhosis (advanced scarring) and fatty liver. The results can help us determine the appropriate course of treatment for the individual and monitor the effectiveness of any treatment.

In addition, we also provide other diagnostic tests and treatments, such as blood tests, imaging tests, and medications. The specific components of a liver care program will depend on the individual’s specific needs and the recommendations of the healthcare team.

Metabolism

Weight Management Program

At Valor Clinic, we focus on the diagnosis and treatment of disorders related to the body’s metabolism, which is the process by which the body converts food into energy. This can include conditions such as diabetes, which is a disorder that affects the body’s ability to produce or use insulin, a hormone that regulates blood sugar levels.

Other conditions that may be treated as part of metabolic care include obesity, metabolic syndrome, and hyperlipidemia, which is an excess of lipids (fats) in the blood. In addition to medications, our weight management program may also involve lifestyle changes such as diet and exercise and the standard FDA approval medications to help manage these conditions and improve overall health.
