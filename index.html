Look Beyond Your Skin

01 · Skin

Skin, scalp and nails

Psoriasis is inflammation across the whole body. It shows most on the skin as red patches with thick scales, heavy flakes on the scalp and changes in the nails.

Dermatology Services
Assoc. Prof. Premjit Juntongjin and Dr. Tin Rerkchuchit
02 · Joints

Psoriatic arthritis

30% of psoriasis patients also have inflamed joints.

Dermatology Services
03 · Heart

Heart and blood vessels

Psoriasis raises the risk of heart and blood vessel disease and stroke. Our board-certified cardiologist follows your heart with an S-Patch that records heart rate and ECG wherever you go.

Cardio-Monitoring Services
Dr. Akanit Srisukwattana, cardiologist
04 · Liver

Fatty liver

Fatty liver is more common with psoriasis. We check liver stiffness and fat with FibroScan™, a non-invasive ultrasound test.

Liver Care Services
Dr. Warittha Worapienkul, gastroenterologist
05 · Metabolism

Weight, sugar and blood fats

Psoriasis raises the risk of diabetes, high blood pressure, high blood fats and obesity. Obesity drives inflammation and makes psoriasis more severe and harder to treat.

Weight Management Program
06 · Mind

Depression and anxiety

Stress, shame and people around them who don't understand weigh on many patients with psoriasis. Care at Valor Clinic looks after both body and mind.

Patient stories

The more severe the psoriasis, the higher the risk of these conditions. Controlling psoriasis well lowers that risk.
