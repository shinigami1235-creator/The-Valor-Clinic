Dermatology Services

At Valor Clinic, we have a board-certified dermatologist who has more than 20 years’ experience in Dermatology. Our dermatologist has completed specialized training from both local and international countries.

Aesthetic Services

At Valor Clinic, all aesthetic treatments are performed by a dermatologist who has been certified with both Thai Board of Dermatology and American Board of Anti-Aging Medicine. We aim to improve the appearance of the skin with the concept of ‘Less is More’, which means sometimes, less intensive or less aggressive treatment can be more effective.

Nevertheless, with the ultimate approach of personalized medical treatment, we believe that there is no a one-size-fits-all approach, and the best course of treatment will depend on the individual patient’s specific needs and circumstances.

Valor clinic offers the premium aesthetic treatments including

  • Topical medications
  • Oral medications
  • Vitamin supplements
  • Botulinum toxin injection
  • Skin booster injection
